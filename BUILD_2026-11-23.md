## 2. Known gaps / limitations

- **OSM coverage in Chhattisgarh is thin.** Many villages have no farmland, buildings or trees mapped. The ground falls back to a default spray area, and the satellite picture is visual only (no collisions). Idea: let the user draw extra trees/buildings on the map picker.
- **Real-map ground size:** the physics box is 160 × 190 m and the satellite picture is 400 × 400 m. Larger fields would need a bigger coverage grid (`GRID`) and minimap bounds.
- **Crop canopy** is a rectangle (field bounding box) for the radar, not the real `fieldPoly`.
- **Phone camera** is MJPEG, about 12 fps at 640×360. It is fine on LAN but uses roughly 300 KB/s. Lower the quality if the hotspot is slow.
- **Phone screen has no login.** Anyone with the QR link (an unguessable token, expires after 8 h) can view and send commands. Acceptable on a private hotspot; add a PIN if used on shared Wi-Fi.
- **Fonts:** menus use the system Arial Black (offline). A bundled display font would look better (see the design-hook note).
- **Old flight logs** (before login existed) belong to no user; only admins see them.
- `start-sim.ps1` firewall migration replaces the old Private-only rule; this has not been run on the owner's PC yet.

## 3. Next features (from the idea list, not started)

1. **Lessons + checkride mode:** hover box, figure-8, nose-in, manual spray lanes, emergency landing; pass/fail limits, instructor sign-off, certificate PDF.
2. **Instructor live panel:** see every trainee's flight on the LAN; inject wind/failures remotely (the relay in `server-live.mjs` can carry it).
3. **Flight replay:** 3D playback of a log with a timeline and stick overlay; compare two trainees.
4. **Camera-drone intelligent flights:** Orbit/POI, waypoints, Dronie/Rocket QuickShots for wedding training.
5. **More failures:** weakening ESC, blocked nozzle, compass interference near power lines, GPS multipath near trees, weak battery cell.
6. **Terrain:** slopes, bunds, canals; radar terrain following. Elevation could come from free SRTM tiles.
7. **ArduPilot `.bin` import** to tune the physics against real AG365 flights.
8. **Real AG365XP spec sheet** → `src/data/drones.ts` (mass, props, thrust, battery). Still estimated.
9. **Hindi / Chhattisgarhi voice prompts.**
10. **Pre-flight checklist screen** (props, nozzles, NOTAM / no-fly zone, wind) that is scored.
11. **Weather by time of day** (wind picks up after 11 am; no spraying above 5 m/s).
12. **VR / head tracking** for the VLOS view.

